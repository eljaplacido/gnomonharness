# Findings, 2026-11-04: long tasks, hybrid on current builds, causal analysis

*Cut back to gnomon-only results on 2026-11-12 for v0.5.0. The November 2026
peer comparisons this note first carried are not published; the study
summaries are archived outside this repository.* Each study was pre-registered
before its data; the commits of the pre-registration and of the result are
given. n is small throughout. "Within one task" and "not shown" mean no
difference was shown, never that the arms are equivalent.

## 1. Long Terminal-Bench 4.0 tasks: why gnomon lost, and how far that is fixed

P2G attempt 1 (gnomon v0.3.1; deepseek-v4.1-flash via OpenCode Go; 1,800 s
cap): gnomon solved 1/29.

| study | question | result | commits |
|---|---|---|---|
| root cause | why 1/29? | v0.3.1's unstreamed calls were cut at 300 s and re-sent (fixed in v0.4.0, #114/#122). On v0.4.0 the remaining symptom was long steps. The guard refused nothing in ~700 calls | — |
| `c2d-2026-11` | what makes steps long? | Wire capture: gnomon never replayed the model's `reasoning_content`; its idle nudge fired from call 11 and stayed in history; its context was capped by the 65,536 default window (the model's is 1,000,000). Single-call replay on 12 recorded long steps: **no direct effect** of sampling, replay or removing nudges (all CIs cross 1×) | e96f30ff → 1444f779 |
| `h1-2026-11` | does a recipe (replay on, 1M window, idle nudge off) close the gap? | 7 paired tasks: timeouts **3 → 0**, output per call about halved, median wall 1,213 → 734 s; solved 2 vs 1 (within one), hidden tests 137 vs 142 of 161 | 55121fe8 → 6146f02c |
| `h2-2026-11` | does a verify-every-requirement rule turn fast finishes into solves? | No: recipe 2/12, recipe + rule 2/12 | b6c26827 → 53ec4990 |

Reading: attempt 1's result combined a fixed transport defect with gnomon's
own long-task settings. The recipe shipped opt-in in v0.4.1
(`replay_reasoning`, `max_context_tokens = "auto"` or a declared window,
`nudge_after_idle`). Its largest part, reasoning replay on hosted endpoints,
became a default by H3's pre-registered rule and is in v0.5.0.

## 3. Causal analysis of the long-task mechanisms (`causal-2026-11`)

With the causal platform's `causal-core` 0.13 and `causal-decision` (plan committed before any estimate; H1 held out):
reasoning replay, measured locally (M1a3, Glimmer): solved +0.23 [−0.005, +0.46]; delivering steering to the model
(S1): solved −0.12 [−0.37, +0.13]; the context window: refused (never varied). Every hosted-lane estimate was refused
with a typed reason. `causal-decision` verdict for each candidate default change: **experiment**. Paired sizing
(causal platform #107) puts a 10-point nudge test at about 110 task pairs. Commits 1ee43c62 → becdd75c.

## 4. What changed in gnomon because of this (v0.4.1)

Opt-in or record-only unless stated: `max_context_tokens = "auto"` (#137), per-call mediator records and an opt-in
calibrated token estimator (#144), a turn record that survives a kill (#143), log-only stall signals (#140; the idle
nudge fires on about half of runs that succeed anyway), and guard fixes found by property tests (#136, #141; tightening).
On a default surface the requests a model receives are byte-identical to v0.4.0 (release gate, #145).
